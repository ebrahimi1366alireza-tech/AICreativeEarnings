# Global earning-channel review — 2026-11-26

This review is global, not limited to Armenia. Eligibility must be evaluated separately for current residence (Armenia) and citizenship/identity document (Iranian passport). It is a research snapshot, not a claim that an account, application, contract, task award, or payment exists.

## Hard qualification rules

- No mandatory deposits, purchases, registration fees, subscriptions, or paid credits.
- Payout must be crypto directly to the user's own wallet. Do not count bank transfer, PayPal, gift cards, or platform balances as qualifying payout methods.
- Verify both Armenia-presence eligibility and Iranian-passport acceptance independently. A country marked Global is not proof that this exact user can pass onboarding or payout checks.
- Verify KYC stage, accepted document types, sanctions screening, wallet network, minimum withdrawal, fees/gas, and actual task availability from official sources.
- No VPN or false country/identity information. Do not create accounts, submit applications, publish social posts, connect/sign wallets, or accept legal terms without the user's explicit approval.
- Prioritize fixed-scope creative work (AI image/video, design, visual assets, editing, and content) with clear acceptance criteria. Exclude food, restaurant, menu, and cooking work.
