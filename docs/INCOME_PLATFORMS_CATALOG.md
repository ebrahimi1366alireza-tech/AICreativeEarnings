# Income platforms and opportunity research catalog

This is a starting catalog for AI Creative Earnings and future income-oriented projects. It is not a promise that any platform accepts the user, pays in every country, or offers currently available work. Re-check official terms before use.

## Freelance services and client work

| Platform | Official site | Intended use | Checks required before relying on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upwork | https://www.upwork.com/ | Freelance projects and client contracts | Country eligibility, identity checks, connects/bidding costs, service fees, and supported withdrawal methods |
| Fiverr | https://www.fiverr.com/ | Sell packaged creative and AI-assisted services | Country eligibility, seller onboarding, service fees, payout methods, and whether the service category is permitted |

## Operating rules

1. The user's preference is genuinely free entry: no mandatory deposit, no paid signup, no refundable-fee trick, and no required purchase to unlock work.
2. Prioritize AI-assisted creative work and services that can be completed from an Android phone/cloud workspace where practical.
3. Before listing a lead as actionable, verify official terms for the user's current country, KYC, payment rails, fees, minimum withdrawal, and whether a client/task is real.
4. Never imply guaranteed income. Clearly label each lead as **verified actionable**, **needs verification**, or **blocked/unavailable**.
5. Do not automate account creation, identity verification, CAPTCHA, OTP, wallet signatures, bids, publishing, or financial transactions without the user's explicit approval at the relevant step.
6. Add new sites only after checking their official domain and basic legitimacy. Keep evidence/date checked in project research notes.
