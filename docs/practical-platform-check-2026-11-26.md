# Practical platform check — 2026-11-26

This addendum records a second, deeper public-source check of the first-priority platforms. It does not represent account-level onboarding or successful payout testing.

## User eligibility rules applied
- Current residence: Armenia.
- Citizenship / identity document: Iranian passport.
- Accept genuine identity and liveness checks, but do not submit them without explicit approval.
- No deposit, paid subscription, paid credits, required purchase, or paid application.
- Payout must be directly withdrawable as crypto to the user's own wallet. A platform balance or a fiat/PayPal payout does not qualify.
- No VPN, false address, false nationality, or sanctions evasion.

## Findings

### 1. Loopuman — best current payout transparency, but not a creative-job marketplace
Official worker information states:
- Workers can use the web, Telegram, or WhatsApp; no app installation is required.
- Worker receives 80% of the task budget after requester approval.
- Payout is on Celo, with USDm, USDT, or USDC options.
- Minimum withdrawal is V100, stated as USD 1.
- The public task page displays examples such as AI answer evaluation ($0.50), data annotation ($0.75), translation ($1.50), and first-person video capture ($5). The site explicitly says these cards are examples; live task availability depends on the worker and some tasks have skill, equipment, or location requirements.
- Earnings are not guaranteed; rejected submissions do not release payment.
- Public pages say workers in 100+ countries / worldwide, but do not specify whether Iranian passport holders resident in Armenia pass onboarding or sanctions screening.
- Verification may be required depending on the task. No definitive public list of accepted identity documents was found.

Decision: **Priority 1 for a no-cost public onboarding check, conditional on eligibility.** It is useful for microtasks and some video/data tasks, not a proven steady source of paid AI creative commissions. Do not buy VAE; purchased VAE is a platform credit, not cash and is non-refundable except where required by law.

Official sources:
- https://loopuman.com/
- https://loopuman.com/microtasks
- https://loopuman.com/browse-tasks
- https://app.loopuman.com/

### 2. ClipRush.net — interesting creator model, payout details missing
Official .net homepage currently says open beta with a small batch of campaigns. Creators can choose campaigns, make original short videos, publish them on their own social channels, and earn on verified views/clicks. It advertises cash or crypto payouts and says crypto/Web3 campaigns settle in crypto.
Missing public details: supported tokens and chains, creator withdrawal minimum, payout fees, payout timing, KYC/identity document rules, geographic restrictions, and whether a creator can use the service with Iranian citizenship and Armenia residence.
Open beta and a small campaign batch also mean supply is limited and cannot be assumed to provide paid work.

Decision: **Priority 2 for terms/payout clarification only.** Do not produce or publish campaign content until payout token/network, minimum, fees, and eligibility are visible and acceptable.

Important domain distinction:
- https://www.cliprush.net/ advertises cash or crypto.
- https://cliprush.online/how-it-works describes a different product/workflow and says payments are PayPal only. That domain is not wallet-payout eligible and must not be confused with cliprush.net.

### 3. LaborX — crypto rails confirmed, but current suitable job and eligibility not confirmed
Official FAQ confirms Ethereum and BNB Chain payment support, including USDC and USDT. Freelancer fee is 10% per completed job/gig. Premium is not required to apply. A job or gig payment and the freelancer's own wallet setup must be agreed with the client. General statements about global access do not prove eligibility for an Iranian passport holder resident in Armenia.
This pass did not verify a live, funded AI image/video editing/design brief with a clear fixed budget and an employer escrow. Public job search should be filtered to exact creative skills and funded milestones; avoid paid Premium or staking.

Decision: **Priority 3 for finding funded fixed-price briefs.** Apply only after checking employer history, funded escrow/milestones, client payment asset/network, fee, and eligibility.

Official source:
- https://laborx.com/vacancies/blockchain-and-crypto

### 4. Superteam Earn — active opportunities, but legal eligibility is a blocker to resolve first
The current opportunity page displays active content/design/other listings, including:
- “Create Content About KriptoK · 200 USDC Creator Challenge” with a deadline shown 18 days away at time of check.
- “Show us how you use MozaikPay” — 500 USDC, deadline shown 13 days away.
- Other USDC opportunities include Inside the Block and RaptorX, but these may be research/writing rather than visual creative work.
The platform FAQ distinguishes competitive Bounties from Projects (freelance applications). For Superteam/Solana-sponsored awards, winners must complete KYC; for external sponsors, payout is generally to the linked wallet but sponsors may require invoices or KYC.
The current Terms of Use (effective June 24, 2026) say users are ineligible if they are located in, or are citizens or residents of, a jurisdiction where use would be illegal or violate applicable law; users must not be subject to economic sanctions. The platform may charge fees; payments are governed by private agreements with partners, and the platform disclaims liability for partner non-payment. Therefore, “global” listings do not prove that this user qualifies.
No definitive public determination was found for an Iranian passport plus Armenia residence.

Decision: **Priority 4, but hold applications until sponsor/platform eligibility is confirmed.** Prefer a paid Project or a small, relevant bounty; do not spend hours on competitive work with unclear payout.

Official sources:
- https://superteam.fun/earn/
- https://superteam.fun/earn/terms-of-use.pdf
- https://docs.superteam.fun/the-superteam-handbook/community/faqs/superteam-earn-faq

### 5. GIMI — creative fit, but payout rail and IP terms are conditional
The latest terms are dated October 6, 2026. They say:
- Creators submit campaign content and earn according to campaign rules and engagement.
- Minimum payout is currently USD 10.
- Payout fee is currently 5%.
- Payout is processed through third-party providers such as Tipalti; options vary by region and may include bank transfer, PayPal, or USD-denominated USDC where offered.
- Additional identity documentation or tax identification may be requested by the payment partner.
- Users may not use GIMI if resident in or located in a sanctioned country/region or if on a sanctions list; GIMI may refuse or withhold payouts for sanctions compliance.
- The current terms say that once content is approved, the Brand owns it, and campaign-specific terms also govern rights. This makes reviewing each campaign's IP terms essential.
- The USDC payout option is not guaranteed for every region; no evidence was found that Armenia with Iranian passport is accepted by the relevant payout partner.

Decision: **Creative fit but not yet wallet-qualified.** Do not create content until the account's payout methods show direct USDC wallet payout (not an intermediary/fiat route), the payment partner accepts the user's residence and passport, and the campaign IP terms are acceptable. The current terms' 5% fee and $10 minimum apply.

Official source:
- https://www.gimi.co/terms-of-use

- No wallet connected, signed, or funded.

## Evidence caveats
Public pages are not a substitute for an authenticated worker dashboard. A public task example is not proof that a task is live or claimable by this worker. A general “worldwide” statement is not proof of acceptance of both the user's current residence and citizenship. The next decisive test requires the user to view the onboarding/payout screen using true details, without submitting identity documents or accepting new legal terms until the eligibility and payout path are understood.
