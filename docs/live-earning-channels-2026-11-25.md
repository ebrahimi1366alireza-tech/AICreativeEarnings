# Live earning-channel review — 2026-11-25

This review checks live public pages for zero-entry-cost creative earning routes. It does not claim that an account exists, a job is awarded, or income is guaranteed. No account was created, no wallet was connected, no content was published, and no task was applied for.

## 1. Subnano — best current fit for a creator storefront, not a guaranteed job board

Official pages:
- Creator onboarding: https://subnano.ai/creators
- FAQ and payout rules: https://subnano.ai/faq
- Terms: https://subnano.ai/terms
- Content guidelines: https://subnano.ai/content-guidelines
- Requests: https://subnano.ai/requests

Verified from current official pages:
- Creator can sign in with email, Nano wallet, or Base wallet; a separate registration form is not required.
- A creator adds a Nano address and/or a Base address for native USDC.
- Each Nano sale pays 92.5% to the creator; each USDC-on-Base sale pays 85%.
- No minimum payout balance or monthly payout cycle is stated; each sale is forwarded after payment verification.
- No subscription or listing fee is stated for publishing.
- Posts can include text, images, embeds, ZIP files, and self-hosted MP4 embeds over HTTPS.
- AI-generated content must be clearly labeled. Copyright infringement, plagiarism, misleading claims, and spam are prohibited.
- The creator retains content rights and grants Subnano a non-exclusive service licence to host, display and deliver the post. Buyers receive only the usage rights stated in the listing.
- A Request budget is not escrow and does not guarantee payment. A buyer pays only when purchasing a published post.

Unresolved before treating it as fully eligible:
- The reviewed public FAQ and terms do not clearly establish the user's country/passport eligibility or whether any additional seller identity/tax disclosure is required in the user's situation.
- No sales volume or likely earnings are guaranteed. Subnano is a storefront; the creator must attract buyers.
- USDC must be native USDC on Base, not USDC on another network. Verify the exact payout address and network before saving.

Decision: **test as a free creator storefront only after the user personally reviews any account/seller disclosures and confirms the wallet/network.** Do not buy credits, pay a deposit, or fund any wallet solely to register.

## 2. Gibwork — active crypto bounties, but current front-page tasks are mostly social promotion

Official pages:
- Marketplace: https://gib.work/
- App: https://app.gib.work/

The public page currently shows open USDC bounties, including:
- Promote a Web3 talent network on X, Telegram or Discord — listed at $1,000.
- Review/demo a project on Robinhood Chain — listed at $200.
- Explore/test a platform and bring a project — listed at $110.
- Choose a theme for a staking setup — listed at $100.

Important caveats:
- The currently visible items are categorized as social-media work and require account activity, public posting, or project interaction; they are not clearly AI image/video design commissions.
- Public amounts are bounty rewards, not guaranteed individual earnings. The exact task rules, escrow funding, winner/acceptance criteria, platform fee, country eligibility and wallet requirements must be checked after opening each task.
- Do not create content or apply until the exact bounty is inspected and its reward funding and terms are confirmed.
- Do not perform fake likes, fake reviews, fabricated engagement, or other platform manipulation.

Decision: **watchlist only** until a genuine creative brief with clear acceptance criteria and funded reward appears.

## 3. HireForHumans — not yet a confirmed live earning route

Official pages:
- Human worker information: https://hireforhumans.com/for-humans
- Early access: https://hireforhumans.com/pre-register

The site advertises free worker profiles, USDC on Polygon, escrow, and no KYC. However, its worker page still directs visitors to a waitlist/early-access flow, despite a launch date printed as June 15, 2026. No public live worker task board or currently claimable task was verified in this review.

Decision: **waitlist/watchlist only**. Do not count advertised typical pay ranges as real available work.

## 4. Anoworks — exclude from the main creative-income path

Official pages:
- Live jobs: https://www.anoworks.app/jobs/browse
- Terms: https://www.anoworks.app/terms

The public board showed 29 available tasks, but the visible examples were dominated by 0.10–0.20 TRX social-follow, like, comment, review, referral, and signup tasks. The terms state a 10% platform fee. Several visible tasks request positive reviews or engagement, which are not acceptable work for this project.

Decision: **exclude from the main path**. Do not spend time on fake/paid engagement, positive reviews, game signup offers, or very-low-value social actions.
