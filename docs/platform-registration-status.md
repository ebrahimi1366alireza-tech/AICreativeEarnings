## Safety gate before every application
- [ ] Task is currently open and accepts new applicants.
- [ ] Deliverable can be completed with AI assistance and does not require the user to perform a human-only test.
- [ ] No entry fee, deposit, stake, bond, paid credit, or subscription is required.
- [ ] No inaccessible identity, phone, social-account, or country restriction blocks the user.
- [ ] AI use is permitted by the platform and task owner.
- [ ] Scope, deadline, acceptance criteria, and revision limits are clear.
- [ ] Escrow is visibly funded or payment terms provide an acceptable, documented safeguard.
- [ ] Net reward after platform/network fees and estimated time is worthwhile.
- [ ] Any wallet connection/signature, legal acceptance, or payment has explicit user approval.
