# Portfolio starter pack 01 — Restaurant & food creatives

**Status:** concept briefs and production specifications only. No client commissioned this work. No final image/video assets have been generated or published yet.

**Purpose:** create a small, honest portfolio that demonstrates AI-assisted visual production for restaurants, cafés, takeaway brands, and food products. The pack is designed to be produced with free tools where available; do not use a paid model, paid stock asset, or paid export without explicit cost approval.

## Portfolio presentation rules

- Label every concept as **Speculative portfolio concept — not commissioned client work**.
- Do not invent client names, testimonials, campaign results, sales lifts, or customer relationships.
- Use original food styling and generic packaging. Do not imitate a real restaurant's logo, menu, brand identity, or copyrighted campaign.
- Disclose AI assistance where the marketplace, client, or applicable rules require it.
- Before commercial delivery, verify the selected tool's commercial-use rights, model restrictions, watermark/export limits, and the client's asset licences.
- Keep editable source files and export a clean preview plus the agreed final formats.
- Never submit an image with malformed food, unreadable text, fake product claims, or inconsistent branding.

## Concept A — Premium Persian grill hero visual

**Use case:** restaurant homepage banner, delivery-app feature image, or menu promotion.

**Creative direction:** a premium but believable overhead/three-quarter food photograph featuring grilled saffron chicken and koobideh-style skewers, saffron rice, grilled tomato, fresh herbs, sumac, and a restrained ceramic table setting. Warm natural side light, appetizing texture, realistic portions, uncluttered composition. Keep one clear focal dish and leave negative space for later typography.

**Image-generation prompt (English):**

> Create an original, photorealistic editorial food photograph for a fictional Persian grill restaurant. A carefully plated serving of saffron rice with grilled chicken and koobideh-style minced kebab skewers, charred tomato, fresh herbs, sumac, and a small bowl of grilled pepper. Warm natural window light from the side, subtle steam, realistic food texture, premium restaurant photography, restrained neutral ceramic tableware, dark warm stone surface, appetizing but natural colors, three-quarter overhead camera angle. Compose the plate slightly to the right and leave clean negative space on the left for a designer to add text later. No text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no packaging, no extra plates, no distorted utensils.

**Deliverables:**
- 1 master image, landscape 16:9, ideally 1920×1080 or larger if the free tool allows.
- 1 square crop, 1080×1080.
- 1 vertical crop, 1080×1350.
- Keep text out of the generated image; add typography later in a layout tool.

**Quality checklist:** kebab texture looks cooked rather than plastic; rice grains and saffron look plausible; plate and utensils are structurally sound; no duplicated food; no accidental lettering; all crops preserve the dish.

**Sample caption:**

> A Persian grill classic, prepared with fragrant saffron rice, flame-grilled kebab, and fresh herbs.  
> *Fictional portfolio concept. Menu details and availability would be confirmed with the restaurant.*

## Concept B — Social launch set for a fictional neighbourhood café

**Use case:** Instagram/Facebook launch announcement and weekly promotion.

**Visual system:** one consistent palette (cream, charcoal, muted pomegranate red), one display typeface paired with a readable sans serif, generous margins, and a repeatable photo treatment. Use generic café imagery and no real logos.

**Three-post sequence:**
1. **Opening soon:** hero coffee-and-pastry still life, headline added in the design tool: “A new neighbourhood table is coming”.
2. **What’s on the table:** three-card menu teaser with generic categories (coffee, breakfast, fresh bakes). Do not invent prices, allergens, ingredients, or availability.
3. **Visit us:** clean location/contact placeholder layout. Keep address, opening date, and contact fields visibly marked “Add confirmed details” until a real client supplies them.

**Image prompt (English):**

> Original editorial photograph for a fictional independent neighbourhood café: a ceramic cup of cappuccino beside a freshly baked croissant on a small wooden café table, soft morning window light, understated cream and charcoal styling with a subtle muted pomegranate-red accent in a napkin, candid premium hospitality photography, natural crumbs and realistic foam, shallow depth of field, clean composition with generous negative space, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no visible brand packaging.

**Deliverables:**
- 3 square posts, 1080×1080.
- 3 vertical story adaptations, 1080×1920.
- 1 reusable editable layout with replaceable photo, headline, date, and CTA fields.

**Copy examples (placeholders, not factual business claims):**
- “A new neighbourhood table is coming.”
- “Your coffee break, thoughtfully made.”
- “Opening details coming soon.”

**Quality checklist:** text contrast and spelling; consistent margins; safe story-area placement; no fabricated address, opening date, offer, or price; the three posts look like one campaign.

## Concept C — 15-second vertical food promo storyboard

**Use case:** short-form restaurant reel or takeaway product clip.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 15 seconds, captions added in the editor, music only from a properly licensed library or original audio.

| Time | Shot | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Close-up of kebab grilling; brief flame and smoke detail | “Fire. Flavour. Freshly prepared.” |
| 3–6 s | Saffron rice plated beside grilled tomato and herbs | “A Persian grill favourite” |
| 6–10 s | Finished plate hero shot with slow camera push | “Made for the moments around the table” |
| 10–13 s | Clean tabletop scene, subtle steam, no people required | “Explore the menu” |
| 13–15 s | Neutral end card with editable brand/contact placeholders | “Your restaurant name · Add confirmed CTA” |

**Generation/editing notes:**
- Generate or source each shot independently; do not expect one prompt to produce accurate sequential continuity.
- Keep the dish, plate, garnish, and lighting consistent across shots.
- Use gentle cuts or simple motion; avoid fake flames, excessive transitions, and AI-generated text.
- Add subtitles and CTA manually in the editor.
- Do not use a real restaurant's footage, logo, menu, or music without permission.

**Deliverables:**
- 1 final MP4, 9:16, 15 seconds.
- 1 caption file or transcript.
- 1 cover image, 1080×1920.
- 1 thumbnail crop, 1080×1080.

## Reusable production workflow

1. Confirm the buyer's brief, audience, platform, exact dimensions, copy, deadline, revision count, and usage rights.
2. Check the chosen tool's free quota, account-country access, watermark/export rules, and commercial licence for the exact model and output.
3. Create 2–3 rough directions; do not spend paid credits.
4. Select one direction and produce the master visual.
5. Inspect anatomy/objects, food realism, spelling, layout, brand consistency, and export quality.
6. Prepare agreed crops and formats; keep source files.
7. Send a watermarked or low-resolution preview only if agreed in the contract; deliver final files after the platform's funded escrow or agreed payment protection is confirmed.
8. Record the order, actual delivery, fee, payout asset/network, and payment receipt in AI Creative Earnings. Pending amounts are not income received.
