# Live creative opportunity review — 2026-11-24

This is a time-sensitive research snapshot. No account was created, no follow was made, no wallet was connected, no application or content was submitted, and no legal or financial action was taken.

## GIMI — reclassified as access-by-contact, not an open creator marketplace

Official creator page: https://www.gimi.co/creators

The current official page describes GIMI primarily as creator-network software for brands. It advertises creator campaigns with approval/milestone/CPM/leaderboard rewards and global creator payouts in USDC, bank transfer, or PayPal. The page presents creator onboarding and applications, but does not expose a public self-service creator signup or a public live-campaign board. Access is presented through a demo/book-a-call flow.

Important correction: earlier notes about a $10 minimum withdrawal and 5% withdrawal fee were not confirmed by the current official creator page. Do not rely on those figures until GIMI confirms them in writing or in the creator account.

Decision: **hold / contact-only**. Do not spend time making content or book a call until GIMI confirms:
- whether an individual creator can join without representing a brand;
- whether there are open campaigns accepting new creators;
- whether USDC payouts are available to creators in Armenia and for an Iranian passport holder;
- exact withdrawal minimum, fees, network, identity/tax checks, and content ownership/licensing terms.

All listings below displayed “Submissions Open” and “Global” at the time of the live page review unless otherwise noted. Superteam's current listing pages show prize pools, not guaranteed individual earnings. Winner selection is competitive.

### 2. CapsGame — gameplay review video or X thread
- Link: https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/publish-capsgame-review-for-solana-audience-video-or-x-thread

### Regional listing excluded
- Superteam Ukraine fundraising article: 500 USDG, but listing explicitly says it is open only to people in Ukraine. The user is currently in Armenia, so do not apply.

## Current recommendation

1. Do not use GIMI as an immediate earning source until creator access, campaign availability, Armenia/Iran eligibility, and USDC withdrawal terms are confirmed.
2. Do not treat Superteam prize pools as guaranteed paid commissions. The currently inspected listings are contests with high submission counts or user-dependent gameplay/social requirements.
3. Keep the search focused on fixed-price AI image/video/design orders with clear acceptance criteria, no entry cost, verified payment protection, and a supported direct crypto payout.
4. Before any application, verify the exact task is open, funded, country-eligible, AI-permitted, and withdrawable. User approval remains mandatory for account actions, public posts, legal terms, identity data, wallet signatures, and submissions.
