## 1. Subnano — viable free storefront mechanics, but no confirmed buyer or country/KYC clearance

Official sources:
- Creator onboarding: https://subnano.ai/creators
- FAQ: https://subnano.ai/faq
- Terms: https://subnano.ai/terms
- Public requests: https://subnano.ai/requests
- API quickstart: https://docs.subnano.ai/v1/api/quickstart

Confirmed from the current official creator page and FAQ:
- Creators can sign in using email, a Nano wallet, or a Base wallet; a separate registration form is not required.
- Creator payouts can be sent to a Nano address (XNO) and/or a Base address for native USDC.
- Nano sales pay 92.5% to the creator; USDC sales pay 85%.
- Sales are forwarded individually after payment verification; no minimum payout balance or monthly payout run is stated.
- There is no monthly plan stated. Posts can be free or paid; free posts can receive Nano tips.
- USDC prices have minimums of 0.10 for text-only posts and 0.25 for posts with ZIP attachments.
- A public Request budget is indicative, is not escrowed/reserved, and does not guarantee a purchase.
- AI-assisted/AI-generated content must follow the platform's content rules; label AI-generated content where required and do not claim human experience or expertise that is not real.

Still unknown and must not be assumed:
- Whether an account holder resident in Armenia and/or an Iranian citizen can use the service under current terms and applicable law.
- Whether the platform or any payment provider requires additional identity, sanctions, tax, or address checks in this user's exact case.
- Whether a particular post or request will attract a paying buyer.

Decision: **review-only / possible free storefront**. It is not a confirmed paid order source. Do not pay a registration fee, deposit, or subscription; do not publish until the user approves the exact content, profile details, wallet address and network. Never send seed phrases or private keys. A wallet address is public information, but the user must personally verify it before saving.
