# Audit of saved earning platforms — 2026-11-26

Scope: the original saved 25-platform list plus additional platforms subsequently added to the project source registry. This is an evidence-based triage, not a claim that an account has been opened or that the user has passed KYC. The user's hard requirements remain: current residence Armenia; Iranian passport; real identity/selfie checks acceptable; no deposit, subscription, paid credits, or paid application; and direct crypto withdrawal to a personal wallet. Only fixed-scope creative work (AI image/video, design, editing, ads, and content) is a high-priority fit.

## Executive result

- **Best candidates to test first:** ClipRush.net (cash or crypto advertised; exact withdrawal rails and KYC still unpublished in reviewed material), Loopuman (USDT/USDC on Celo; 80% of task budget to workers; live task access and identity/country checks still need account-level verification), LaborX (crypto payments and 10% freelancer fee; country/KYC and live creative jobs still need checking), Superteam Earn (USDC crypto opportunities; sponsor-specific KYC and payout rules), and GIMI (USDC is one possible payout method, but third-party provider, $10 minimum, 5% fee, and content ownership terms need acceptance).
- **Conditional / not yet qualified:** Humanbased (USDC on Base but beta/invite and campaign/KYC eligibility unknown), Sapien (USDC/token rewards described in tokenomics; live task access, stake requirements, and nationality checks unknown), ClipFlip (verified-view creator campaigns but creator payout currency/rail not confirmed), Scribble (paid creator bounties but public information says cash, not direct wallet crypto), Zealy (USDC rewards possible but campaign/community rewards are not guaranteed), Microworker.app (crypto payouts but explicitly excludes Iran; residence-vs-nationality and passport acceptance unresolved).
- **Exclude or deprioritize under the current rules:** DRiP (needs an active USDC Solana account with a small pre-existing balance), Genesis/OnFin (KYC and referral rewards tied to referred users making deposits; financial-broker promotion), Layer3 (mostly points/quests, not reliable liquid crypto earnings), AI Film Contests as an aggregator (prize currency and eligibility vary by individual contest; several current high-prize contests pay USD rather than crypto), and low-quality engagement/review/faucet tasks that do not meet the creative-work goal.
- **Unverified:** Kyzzen, Airaa, PublicAI, CryptoTask, Dealwork, Bountycaster, AnoWorks, Genesis/OnFin, and several smaller names below need deeper account-level and legal/payout checks before they can be treated as qualified. Some have enough public evidence to classify the payment model, but not enough to verify Iranian passport + Armenia residence.
